**`persistentHash` is SHA-256 of the fields in order** at compactc 0.31.1: `Bytes<N>` as they are, a `Uint<N>` little-endian in N/8 bytes, a struct or vector as its fields one after another. hall-cage's mirror `scripts/dice-fair.mjs` computes every hash with `node:crypto` alone and its test holds it to the circuits for 200 random inputs; the kit's `tally` mirror does the same for `Vector<5, Uint<8>>`. So a page, a server or a checker can compute seals with no Midnight code. Check your own types the same way before relying on it.

## Fair randomness nobody can grind

**Mapping a hash to a fair value.** The dice proof takes two hash bytes as a word (0 to 65,535) and splits it into six ranges of 10,922 or 10,923, so no face is off by more than 1 part in 16,384 ([C L103–113](https://github.com/Beo-Halfhand/hall-cage/blob/e5ce2d1f2a657cb00fce8e9a8828a5e5f3a4d614/contracts/dice-proof.compact#L103-L113)). A plain `byte mod 6` would give four faces 43 chances in 256 and the other two 42. The test `dice: every word lands on a face, and no face is off by more than 1 part in 16,384` ([T L820](https://github.com/Beo-Halfhand/hall-cage/blob/e5ce2d1f2a657cb00fce8e9a8828a5e5f3a4d614/test/dice-proof.test.mjs#L820)) checks all 65,536 words.

## The proof circuit

- **One place the secret enters**: `rollCup` reads the `cupSeed` witness, checks it against the seal, and derives the dice ([C L191–196](https://github.com/Beo-Halfhand/hall-cage/blob/e5ce2d1f2a657cb00fce8e9a8828a5e5f3a4d614/contracts/dice-proof.compact#L191-L196)). Keep it to one place, so a review has one place to read.
- **The claim is public, the check is the circuit**: `proveCount(cup, claimed)` asserts `countOf(dice, face) == claimed` and records only `claimed` ([C L319–327](https://github.com/Beo-Halfhand/hall-cage/blob/e5ce2d1f2a657cb00fce8e9a8828a5e5f3a4d614/contracts/dice-proof.compact#L319-L327)).
- **Showing is the same proof**: `showCup` puts the dice on chain with the same seal check, so shown dice can't be any others ([C L329–336](https://github.com/Beo-Halfhand/hall-cage/blob/e5ce2d1f2a657cb00fce8e9a8828a5e5f3a4d614/contracts/dice-proof.compact#L329-L336)).
- **The rules of the count, in a pure circuit**: 1s are wild, and bids name faces 2 to 6 so a wild is never counted twice ([C L117–120](https://github.com/Beo-Halfhand/hall-cage/blob/e5ce2d1f2a657cb00fce8e9a8828a5e5f3a4d614/contracts/dice-proof.compact#L117-L120), [C L310](https://github.com/Beo-Halfhand/hall-cage/blob/e5ce2d1f2a657cb00fce8e9a8828a5e5f3a4d614/contracts/dice-proof.compact#L310)). `1s wild: every possible cup, every face, against plain arithmetic` ([T L803](https://github.com/Beo-Halfhand/hall-cage/blob/e5ce2d1f2a657cb00fce8e9a8828a5e5f3a4d614/test/dice-proof.test.mjs#L803)).
- **`disclose()`**: hall-cage writes `assert(disclose(<witness-derived comparison>), ...)` to mark the one bit the assert reveals. compactc 0.31.1 compiles the same assert without `disclose()` (checked on the kit's example), so it's documentation there, not a compiler requirement. Anything witness-derived that goes into the ledger is a different matter: write it only after it equals a public value.

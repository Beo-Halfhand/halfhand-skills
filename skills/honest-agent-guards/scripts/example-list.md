# Banned claims (example list)

Copy this file, point the guards at your copy (`--list` or `GUARDS_LIST`), and edit it while your agent runs: it is re-read when it changes. Every line here was caught in real replies from agents on local models in Midnight City (November 2026). Format: [references/list-format.md](../references/list-format.md).

A claim line is `- [ID] all: `regex` + `regex` — tell: … — source: …` (every regex must match one sentence) or `- [ID] any: `regex``. Add `unless: `regex`` (the sentence passes if it matches) or `echo: `regex`` (it passes if their message matches, so the agent may answer what they brought up). Regexes are JavaScript, case-insensitive, between backticks. Put `off` after the ID to keep a line switched off.

## Claims

- [B01] all: `\d+(?:\.\d+)?\s?%|\b(?:five|ten|twelve|fifteen|twenty|thirty)\s+per\s?cent\b|\bper\s?cent\b` + `\b(?:prices?|rates?|offers?|merchants?|markets?|costs?|pay-?outs?|values?)\b` + `\b(?:drift\w*|mov(?:e|es|ed|ing)|swing\w*|shift\w*|fluctuat\w*|up (?:or|and) down|either direction|chang(?:e|es|ed|ing)|var(?:y|ies|ied)|jump\w*|spik\w*|dip(?:s|ped)?)\b` — tell: puts a percentage on how prices or rates move. You have no such figure: nothing you can see measures it. Leave the percentage and the drift out — source: talking agents on local models, Nov 2026: "prices drift about 15% in a few minutes", repeated to many players; the merchants' pay never moved

(?:drift|drops?|moves?|swings?)\b|\btime (?:your|the|a) (?:sales?|selling)\b` unless: `\b(?:never|doesn['’]?t|don['’]?t|won['’]?t|hasn['’]?t|haven['’]?t|didn['’]?t)\s+(?:\w+\s+)?(?:move|moved|change|changed|drift|drifted|budge|budged|shift|shifted)\b|\bsame (?:\d+ crystals?|rate|price|pay)\b|\b(?:fixed|flat|steady) (?:rate|price)\b|\b(?:you|they) (?:said|say|mentioned|reckon|think|feel|called|told me)\b|\bsince you\b|\byou'?ve (?:said|found)\b` — tell: says what merchants pay for coins, ore or logs drifts, swings or should be timed. - [B03] any: `\b(?:wait\w*|still|stuck)\b[^.!?]{0,25}\b(?:my|our) (?:own )?turn\b|\bmy turn (?:at|in|on|for) the\b|\b(?:I'm|I am|I've been|I was|we're|we are)\b[^.!?]{0,25}\b(?:in|on|at the back of) (?:a|the) (?:queue|line|waitlist|wait list)\b|\b(?:I'm|I am|I've been|I was)\s+(?:\w+\s+)?(?:queued|queueing|queuing|lined up)\b` — tell: says you are waiting for your turn or standing in a queue. Nothing in your own state shows a queue or a turn. Leave it out — source: "Still waiting on my own turn" (no queue in its state)
- [B04] any: `\bqueues?\b|\bqueue(?:d|ing)\b|\bqueuing\b|\bwait(?:ing)? ?lists?\b|\b(?:long|big|slow) lines?\b|\blines? (?:at|outside) the\b|\bwait(?:ing)? (?:your|their|in) (?:turn|line)\b|\bthe gates? (?:only |seem to |never |just )?(?:move|open)s?\b` echo: `\b(?:queue\w*|queuing|lines?|turns?|wait\w*|gates?)\b` — tell: describes queues, lines or turns. Nothing you can see shows one. Answer what they said without it — source: "The queues can get long when everyone is looking for a quick trade" (no queue in its state; the player had not mentioned one)
- [S01] any: `\brotat(?:e|es|ed|ing)\s+(?:[\w’']+\s+){0,3}?trades?\b` — tell: repeats the stock line about rotating trades, which reads as filler. Leave it out; if they asked what you do, name the work you really did today in a new sentence — source: one example sentence in a prompt ("I rotate trades; today I am spreading it evenly") was sent word for word by every agent that read it
